OUR MINISTRIES

One Church. Many Expressions.

CRE Ministries is a family where people of different ages and backgrounds can find a place to belong, grow and serve.

Our ministries provide opportunities for fellowship, spiritual growth, discipleship, service and practical expression of our faith.

Every ministry contributes to our greater vision of transforming one billion lives for Christ.
