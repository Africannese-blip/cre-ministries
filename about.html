ABOUT CRE MINISTRIES

A Home of Transformers

Christ Royal Eagles Ministries, commonly known as CRE Ministries, is a place of transformation.

We serve Jehovah Metamaphor — the God who transforms lives, changes destinies and makes all things new.

At CRE Ministries, you are welcome to come as you are. But our desire is that you will not remain the same.

Once you enter the CRE auditorium, transformation begins.
