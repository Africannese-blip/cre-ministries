There Is A Place For You At CRE

At Christ Royal Eagles Ministries, our services are designed to create an atmosphere where people can encounter God, receive His Word, grow in faith and experience transformation.

Whether you are joining us for worship on Sunday, growing through our Tuesday teachings or pressing deeper in prayer on Friday, we believe every gathering is an opportunity to encounter Christ.

You are welcome to come as you are. Our prayer is that as you encounter Christ, your life will be transformed and you will leave ready to influence your sphere.
