WELCOME TO CRE MINISTRIES

A Place of Faith.
A People of Purpose.

Welcome to Christ Royal Eagles Ministries, affectionately known as CRE Ministries — the Home of Transformers.

We are a Christ-centred ministry committed to seeing lives transformed through the power of the Word of God, prayer, worship and the presence of the Holy Spirit.

Our heart is to raise people who know Christ, walk in their purpose and become agents of transformation in their families, communities and nations.
