LEADERSHIP

Leading Through Service

CRE Ministries is led by a team of dedicated servants who work together to advance the vision of the ministry.

Our leadership structure consists of the Founders, Resident Pastors, Elders, Deacons and Ministers.

Together, they serve the church, equip believers and help create an environment where people can encounter Christ and experience transformation.

OUR LEADERSHIP PHILOSOPHY

Leadership is Service

At CRE Ministries, leadership is not about position alone. It is about serving people, developing people and helping people fulfil their God-given purpose.

The leadership of CRE Ministries carries one central vision:

Transforming one billion lives for Christ.
